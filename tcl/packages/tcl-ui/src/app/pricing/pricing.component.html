Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained according to your plan's retention policy. You can export your data before canceling on Team/Developer and Enterprise plans.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on Team/Developer plans. Enterprise plans are custom and include specific terms.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, Team/Developer plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
